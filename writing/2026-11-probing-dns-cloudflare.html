Probing DNS at Cloudflare

source: linkedin — original ↗
published: 2026-11-27 · status: canonical · expanded from the original post

Over the next year, I expect more platform teams to make continuous production probes part of rollout approval, rather than just another dashboard.

Cloudflare’s DNS extension to Flamingo is a useful signal: automated tests run from every data center against authoritative nameservers and public resolvers, checking performance and propagation delays. Detected issues can automatically block rollouts before customers are affected.

The detail that matters is the connection between measurement and deployment control. A DNS check becomes more valuable when it can stop a release, not just explain why requests failed afterward.

For teams modernizing distributed platforms, that also raises a practical question: which probe failures justify blocking a rollout? Coverage matters, but so does avoiding gates that engineers routinely override.

https://blog.cloudflare.com/probing-dns-at-cloudflare?utm_source=tldrit

Signal to watch: Whether probe-driven gates catch DNS regressions early without excessive false blocks. Track blocked rollouts, override frequency, and customer-visible DNS incidents together—not probe volume alone.
