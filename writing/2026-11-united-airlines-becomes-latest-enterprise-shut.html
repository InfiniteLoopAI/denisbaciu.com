published: 2026-11-16 · status: canonical · expanded from the original post

United’s restrictions on unauthorized personal AI agents highlight the risk I’d prioritize: unintended purchases, not model accuracy.

[ ] Establish delegated identity: distinguish the customer from the agent and record exactly which actions the customer authorized.

[ ] Separate browsing from buying: require explicit approval tied to the final price and itinerary before committing a purchase.

[ ] Make transaction retries safe with idempotency keys and clear status responses. Timeouts happen.

[ ] Keep an audit trail linking customer consent, agent actions, and transaction outcomes; avoid exposing account data unnecessarily.

For teams opening transactional systems to agents, these are release criteria—not safeguards to add after the pilot.
